@* Nothing is configured. Time runs the way the page reads: the task list moves to the right,
   the timeline starts at the right edge, and the bars, the weekend shading, the today line and
   the arrows all turn round with it.

   Everything is placed with logical offsets, which mirror on their own. Two things cannot:

     the grid lines, because a repeating gradient needs a physical direction; and
     the arrows, because an SVG coordinate system does not flip with the page, so an arrow
     that still pointed right would be pointing backwards in time.

   Both are handled in the stylesheet, off [dir="rtl"], so there is no parameter to remember
   and no way for the chart to disagree with the page it is on. *@
